What Makes a Good Source Photo?

The quality of the input photo significantly affects avatar output. For best results: use a clear, front-facing portrait where your face fills most of the frame. Good lighting — natural light or soft studio lighting — helps the model accurately capture your features. Avoid heavy filters on the source photo, as these can interfere with the model's face detection. A plain or simple background produces cleaner results than busy environments, although the model handles most backgrounds competently.

Group photos can be used, but the model focuses on the most prominent face in the frame. Sunglasses, masks, or heavy obstructions to the face will reduce output quality, as the model has less facial information to work with.
