Frequently Asked Questions

Are the emotes sized correctly for Twitch and Discord upload?

Yes. The Emoticon Generator outputs transparent PNG files compatible with both Twitch and Discord upload requirements. Both platforms automatically handle resizing on upload, so you can submit the generated file directly without manual resizing. For the clearest results at small display sizes, include "bold outline, flat cartoon style" in your prompt to ensure the emote reads clearly at emoji scale.

Can I use generated stream assets commercially — on Twitch, YouTube, and merch?

Yes. Assets generated and processed with Deep Vortex AI are yours for commercial use, including monetized Twitch streams, YouTube channel art, and merchandise. What format are stream graphics and emotes delivered in?

Emotes and background-removed assets are delivered as transparent PNG — the correct format for Discord server emotes, Twitch channel emotes, and stream overlay graphics. The Image Generator and Image to Video tool output PNG and MP4 respectively, both ready for direct upload to streaming platforms, social media, or import into OBS and streaming software.
