Frequently Asked Questions

Everything about our AI 3D model generator.

What is a GLB file?

GLB is a binary 3D file format (part of the glTF standard) compatible with Unity, Unreal Engine, Blender, Three.js, AR Quick Look, and most 3D applications.

What images work best for 3D generation?

Images with a single subject on a plain or transparent background give the best results. Product photos, character art, and objects with clear silhouettes work especially well.

Can I use the 3D models in a game engine?

Yes. GLB files are directly importable into Unity, Unreal Engine, Godot, Blender, and web frameworks like Three.js and Babylon.js.
