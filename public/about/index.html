Our Mission

"To make professional-grade AI creative tools accessible to everyone — from solo creators to small businesses — with transparent pay-per-use pricing and no mandatory subscriptions. We believe powerful AI should not require a $40/month commitment."

Who We Are

Deep Vortex AI was founded in 2024 in Quebec, Canada with a single belief: the barrier to accessing frontier AI tools is too high. While major AI platforms charge $10–$30 per month per tool, most users only need to generate a handful of images, remove a background, or clone a voice on demand — not on a monthly cycle.

We built a platform where nine AI tools share a single credit balance. You generate images, animate them into video, remove the background, upscale the result, and synthesize a voice-over — all from one account, paying only for what you actually use. Credits never expire.

Since launch, Deep Vortex AI has reached users in over 200 countries. Every tool runs on the latest available AI models — Flux for image generation, leading TTS models for voice, and frontier models including Claude Sonnet 4.5, GPT-5, Gemini 2.5 Pro, and DeepSeek v3 for our AI Chat platform.
